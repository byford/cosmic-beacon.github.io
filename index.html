02 Wind

The anemometer wrote 648,392 readings, every one of them with a clean status byte — not a single fault frame all week. The mast sat through two distinct weathers: a still opening weekend, then three days that blew hard from Tuesday to Thursday.

04 Position

The receiver logged 1,013,613 fixes. It never moved — which is the point: the spread of those fixes around their own median is the receiver's noise, and it is a good way to say how well the sculpture knew where it was.

05 Temperature

Nothing wrote temperatures to /data. These come from the hub's journal, one flat line every five minutes, and they are board and enclosure temperatures — not the air on the playa. What they answer is whether a sealed box on a tower gets too hot to work, and it did not.
